POD Go Companion

Back up and restore the presets on your POD Go from the browser, including snapshots 5 and 6 when the unit runs the six-snapshot firmware.

Before you connect. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer and quit POD Go Edit and Line 6 Updater: only one program can talk to the POD Go at a time. A backup loads each preset on the unit in turn, so save any changes on the POD Go first: unsaved edits are lost when the next preset loads.

Unofficial and experimental. Not made or supported by Line 6. Restoring overwrites presets on the POD Go: keep a backup you trust before you restore. Back to the setup page.

1. Connect

Not connected.

2. Back up

Reads every preset of the current setlist and saves them in one file on this computer. Takes a minute or two; the POD Go steps through its presets and returns to the one you were on.

  • With the six-snapshot firmware, presets that use snapshots 5 and 6 are saved with them (marked 5·6).
  • Nothing is uploaded: the file stays on this computer.
Save the backup file
#PresetSizeSnapshots 5 and 6

3. Restore

Open a backup file made on this page, then put one preset back into any slot, or restore the whole backup. Each restored preset is saved on the POD Go, snapshots 5 and 6 included.
